With a passion for technology dating back to the 1980s, Steven Debruyn is an experienced cybersecurity professional with over 30 years of expertise at the intersection of technology, security, and human behavior. As Domain Lead in Cybersecurity Awareness at the NMBS, he develops large-scale awareness programs for over 20,000 employees, with a strong focus on human-centered risk management and strengthening organizational resilience.

His career began in the pioneering days of computing, when he assembled and sold his first custom-built PCs in the 1980s. Later, he built extensive expertise in combating cybercrime and led anti-piracy operations in collaboration with Interpol and various police forces. Thanks to this unique combination of technical depth and strategic insight, Steven became a respected voice in the field of cybersecurity awareness and behavioral change.

He delivers inspiring and interactive keynotes, workshops, and training programs to a wide range of organizations and institutions, including ENISA, CERT, the European Commission, universities, the Flemish government, and the Belgian Federal Foreign Affairs Ministry.

Furthermore, Steven is an active member of the Belgian Cyber Security Coalition and is committed to strengthening cyber resilience within both public and private organizations. Through his multilingual abilities and his capability to translate technical complexity into clear insights for both employees and leaders, Steven helps organizations build a strong and sustainable security culture from within.
